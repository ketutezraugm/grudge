# Identity (locked)

PLAYBOOK Phase 3 asks for this file. The detailed spec is [brand-guidelines.md](brand-guidelines.md); this records what is decided and what is not.

**Direction: Grudge Book.** An accountant's ledger where every wrong call is written down in red pencil. Paper, ink, red pencil, hand-built SVG marks (G-gauge, tally marks, strikes, stamps), ruled rows. It replaced a dark and neon v1 on 2026-11-07 after the owner rejected that look, and on 2026-11-08 the owner chose it again as the starting point for Claude Design, which may push it but not replace it.

| Part | Decision | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name, motto | Grudge. Markets forget. Grudge doesn't. | brand-guidelines §Quick Reference |
| Emotion | The satisfaction of "I told you so", backed by receipts | §Quick Reference |
| Voice | Petty, deadpan, precise; never mean to people, only to bad calls | §5 |
| Palette | Paper #F4EFE4, Ink #1E1A16, Red pencil #C2361F, Stamp green #2E6A4C, Ink blue #22385C, Ledger rule #B9C7D3. Colour means right or wrong, never direction | §2 |
| Type | A ledger serif for display, a tabular mono or old-style tabular face for numbers, a plain text face for UI. All Google Fonts under the OFL | §3 |
| Art direction | Hand-built marks, paper grain and ink spread, no stock art or icon packs | §4 |
| Motion | Needle eases 600 ms; strikes and tallies draw in 250 ms; stamps press in 180 ms; reduced motion switches all of it off | §6 |
| Bans | Playbook section 6, in full | DESIGN-BRIEF §9 |

**Not decided here, left to Claude Design:** the final typefaces within those constraints, every layout, and how the marks are drawn.
